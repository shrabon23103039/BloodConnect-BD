How BloodConnect BD works

The goal is to make donor discovery and contact more organized while keeping donor choice and privacy at the centre.

  1. 1

    Search by blood group and area

    People will be able to filter by an exact blood-group label and broad location. These are discovery filters only; they do not assess medical compatibility.

  2. 2

    Send a contact request

    A requester asks to connect. The donor's private contact details remain hidden while the request is pending.

  3. 3

    Let the donor choose

    The donor can accept or decline. Acceptance may reveal only contact methods the donor explicitly chose to share. It does not mean that a donation occurred.

Current build status: this repository is still building the platform foundation. Search, accounts and contact requests are not available yet. No real donor data should be entered.
