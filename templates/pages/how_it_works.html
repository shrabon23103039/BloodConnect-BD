How BloodConnect BD works

The goal is to make donor discovery and contact more organized while keeping donor choice and privacy at the centre.

  1. 1

    Search by blood group and area

    Find potential contacts using an exact blood-group label and supported broad administrative areas. These filters do not assess medical compatibility.

  2. 2

    Send a contact request

    A requester sends a contact request with context. The donor's private contact details remain hidden while the request is pending.

  3. 3

    Let the donor choose

    The donor can accept or decline. Acceptance may reveal only contact methods the donor explicitly chose to share. It does not mean that a donation occurred.

Current build status: accounts, donor profiles and consent-led contact requests are available. Find Donors currently returns results using the supported administrative filters; nearby radius matching is not available yet. Use synthetic data in this development build.
