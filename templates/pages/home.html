A clear, consent-led process

Three steps to start a conversation

BloodConnect BD helps people coordinate contact. It does not arrange or guarantee a donation.

  1. 01

    Find potential donors

    Filter by the exact blood group and a broad area. Only visible, available profiles with current discovery consent are shown.

  2. 02

    Request contact

    Send a contact request with relevant context. A request does not reveal a donor's private phone number or email.

  3. 03

    The donor decides

    The donor can accept or decline. After acceptance, only the contact methods they chose to share may be shown.

Important to know

Potential contacts, not medical advice

Results are potential contacts only. Medical suitability and compatibility must be assessed by an authorized healthcare professional. Donor availability is self-reported and does not guarantee a response.
