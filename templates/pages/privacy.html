Privacy

Privacy starts with donor choice

This is a draft description of the planned MVP data practices, not a final legal privacy notice.

Before public launch: a qualified local reviewer must approve the lawful basis, retention periods, user rights, processors and contact details for the final notice. Those details are not yet defined in the project documents.

Information the planned service may use

To support accounts and coordination, the MVP may process account identifiers, user-provided donor profile details, requested blood group and broad location, availability preferences, contact requests and moderation reports. Donor profile and availability information is self-reported.

What is public

Public donor discovery is planned to show only fields the donor consented to make visible, such as a chosen display name, blood group, generalized area and self-reported willingness to receive requests. Phone numbers, email addresses, exact home addresses, precise coordinates, identity documents and private notes must not appear in public results.

When contact information may be shared

A recipient must send a contact request. The donor must explicitly accept before the service reveals any contact channel, and it may reveal only the channel the donor chose to share.

Data handling and your choices

Signed-in account holders can change purpose-specific privacy choices in Privacy settings. Each recorded grant or withdrawal includes a policy version and timestamp; previous consent events are retained as history. Donor discovery and consent-led contact requests are implemented. Donor contact channels remain private until the donor accepts a request, and only channels selected for sharing may then be disclosed. Retention, deletion, access-request and complaint procedures must be documented before real personal or health-related information is collected. This build should use synthetic data only; no real donor data should be entered.

Contact

A privacy contact address has not been configured. It must be published in the final notice before launch.
