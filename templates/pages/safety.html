Safety and limitations

Use BloodConnect BD as a way to connect

This platform supports communication between people. It does not provide healthcare or decide who can donate.

Not an emergency service. BloodConnect BD cannot provide urgent medical help, guarantee that a donor will respond or guarantee blood availability. For urgent situations, contact local emergency services and a licensed healthcare professional.

Medical decisions belong to healthcare professionals

Results are potential contacts only. Medical suitability and compatibility must be assessed by an authorized healthcare professional. Blood group and location filters are discovery tools; they do not determine compatibility, eligibility or safety.

Availability is self-reported

An “available” status means only that a person has said they are willing to receive contact requests. It is not medical clearance and does not promise a response or donation.

Respect consent and privacy

Do not seek or share another person's private contact information without permission. Donor contact channels stay private until the donor accepts a request, and only explicitly consented channels may then be disclosed.

What the platform does not do

BloodConnect BD does not collect, test, store, sell, transport or transfuse blood. It is not a blood bank, hospital, laboratory or medical provider.

This information requires review by qualified local healthcare and legal professionals before public launch.
